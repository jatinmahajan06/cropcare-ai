How it works

A simple four-stage AI pipeline.

Capture

Farmer photographs an affected leaf with a phone.

Pre-process

Image is resized, normalised and color-segmented.

AI Prediction

A CNN model classifies the symptoms and scores confidence.

Advice

System returns severity, treatment and prevention tips.

Note: this demo runs a lightweight in-browser color/lesion analysis to illustrate the workflow. The production design uses a CNN (e.g. MobileNetV2 / ResNet) trained on the PlantVillage dataset.
